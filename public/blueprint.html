PHASE 23 Admin Logistics

Automated Courier Logistics Bridge & Bulk Dispatch

Connects directly with Steadfast, Pathao, and RedX APIs. Replaces hours of manual order copying with 1-click bulk booking and automated webhook reconciliation.

  • 1-Click Bulk Booking: Select 50 orders on the Orders table and dispatch simultaneously.
  • Automated AWB Generation: Instant courier consignment IDs without external portal visits.
  • Bulk Thermal Sticker Labels: Generates 4-per-page or thermal barcode shipping slips.
  • Inbound Webhook Sync: Real-time delivery webhook auto-marks orders as Delivered and clears COD cash.
app/Services/Logistics/CourierManager.php
PHASE 24 Warehouse & POS

Warehouse Barcode Scanner & Quick POS Counter Mode

Eliminates packing mistakes (wrong size/color sent) and provides a fast physical retail counter checkout linked to the same real-time inventory ledger.

  • Scan-to-Pack Verification: Staff scans invoice barcode, then beeps each garment. Turns green only when 100% verified.
  • Fast POS Touch Register: Instant barcode gun beep checkout with split-tender payment (Cash + Card + bKash).
  • Silent 80mm Thermal Print: Direct receipt output without print dialog interruptions.
  • Code-128 Barcode Sticker Generator: Generates printable adhesive sticker sheets for apparel tags.
app/Http/Controllers/Admin/PosController.php
PHASE 25 Loss Prevention

COD Fraud Detection & Smart Risk Scoring Guard

Protects the business against prank orders, competitor sabotage, and habitual returners causing expensive courier return fees. Evaluates risk in real time upon checkout.

  • Customer Delivery Trust Score: Computes historical success rate across past orders.
  • Advance Delivery Charge Gate: Low-trust customers prompted to pay shipping fee (৳70/৳130) before confirmation.
  • Automated Bot & Duplicate Detector: Flags repeat orders placed within 15 mins or throwaway emails.
  • 1-Click Blacklist Directory: Ban fraudulent phone numbers or IP addresses directly from Orders view.
app/Services/Fraud/OrderRiskEvaluationService.php
